About the Prototype

This Build 1 demonstration recognizes only a few predefined contradiction patterns in short excerpts. It cannot perform a comprehensive manuscript review.

Privacy: the prototype processes text in your browser and does not upload or save your manuscript. Avoid entering confidential material on untrusted devices.
